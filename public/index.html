Why we opened

Some conversations need a table, not a screen.

Kadhaipooma started as one table on a college veranda where a few of us traded stories over kaapi instead of scrolling. We kept the habit and built a whole cafe around it.

"Every table here has heard something it will never repeat."

What you'll find

  • Filter kaapi made the slow way, no shortcuts.
  • A corkboard wall where regulars leave notes for strangers.
  • Corners built for long conversations, not fast turnover.
  • Snacks meant for sharing across the table.
