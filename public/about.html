Our story

Why Kadhaipooma exists

Kadhaipooma began on a college veranda, with three friends and one flask of filter kaapi, trading stories instead of scrolling through our phones. We noticed how rare that had become — a table with nothing on it but cups and conversation.

So we built a cafe around that one habit. No screens on the tables. No music loud enough to compete with a good story. Just kaapi, snacks meant for sharing, and a corkboard wall that keeps growing.

"Kadhaipooma" means "shall we tell a story?" — it's less a name than an invitation.

Every table has heard something it will never repeat. The wall is where we keep the ones people wanted to pass on.

What we care about

  • Slowness — kaapi brewed the old way, nobody rushing your table.
  • Honesty — the wall keeps stories as people wrote them, unedited.
  • Belonging — regulars and strangers share the same bench.
  • Memory — some of these stories are the only record left of a person or a place.

A note on the wall

The Story Wall is public and unmoderated by design — we only remove what's harmful. Please write with care for the strangers who'll read it next.
